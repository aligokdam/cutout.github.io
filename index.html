    Cutout Effect Studio

    Turn any photo into bold, flat, poster-style art.

    This tool takes a photo and reduces it to a small number of flat color tones with clean, simplified edges — the classic "cutout" or posterized look, similar to a paper collage or screen print.

    How to use it

    1

    Upload a photo — click the upload area, drag & drop, or press Enter.

    2

    Adjust Number of Levels, Edge Simplicity and Edge Fidelity to shape the effect.

    3

    Use Reset to Original or Reset All Settings any time to start over.

    4

    Pick PNG or JPG and download your result.

    Privacy: Everything happens in your browser. Your photo never leaves your device and is never stored on a server.
